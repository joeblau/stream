# Recording private video ingress

Recording now admits actual private video pools before constructing a segment's writers. One synchronous batch includes the compositor's actual source bank, Program's private bank, and every selected ISO's actual renderer source and private bank. The compositor adopts its moved child receipt at the actor installation acknowledgment; recorder cleanup cannot close that shared source pool.

The existing queue policies stay intact. With actual private-buffer `CVPixelBufferGetDataSize` D, Program queues Q = max(1, min(30, floor(128,000,000 / D))) frames. Its bank contains Q+5 buffers: two completed broker deliveries, one callback/incoming overlap, one dequeued native append, and one copy in flight. ISO queues Q = max(1, min(6, floor(48,000,000 / D))) and uses Q+2 for its native append and incoming closed copy. Each ISO also uses an independently admitted four-buffer SceneRenderer source bank. Reservations charge the larger measured backing bytes, including stride/IOSurface allocation, rather than assuming D is the complete allocation size.

Program callbacks receive broker-private samples and forward a typed sample plus exact bank to the writer. ISO rendering uses its admitted source bank, then finishes a row-aware private copy before returning a writer sample. Raw compatibility callers copy into a lazily admitted bank before the first native writer configuration. No attachment or IOSurface identity registry is used to infer trusted provenance. BGRA/alpha, color metadata, PTS, DTS and duration use the shared converter contract; the bounded `RecordingChatPaint` attachment is explicitly decoded/re-encoded despite ShouldNotPropagate.

The original first queued frame stays pinned until the first accepted native append. Queue/encoder drops and invalid-PTS inlet drops retain their existing counters. Admission, occupied private capacity, conversion, provenance and broker dispositions have separate typed progress. Broker total drops overlap its reason counters; these are not additive frame totals. Stop and rotation synchronously cancel their owned ticket, collect the frozen per-lease removal receipt, and apply it before writer finalization publishes the terminal journal. Same-token replacement retains the old lease's final receipt and cannot remove the replacement. A late old-bank callback cannot resolve a new segment's boundary. Pause arriving during a handoff is latched until the new writer's actual recording event. A Stop boundary cancellation arriving before delayed finish remains latched on the writer queue.

Rotation admits the old and new banks concurrently before the second writer is constructed. Admission failure leaves the existing segment running; an unconfirmed/failed actor handoff stops that recorder with a visible error. An exposed bank stays fully charged and its allocator closes on retirement. Native completion, callback return, wrapper destruction and bare IOSurface/Metal aliases do not justify reusing credits or backing in a fresh session. Finite occupied private banks shed that output's transfers; this does not establish a bound on a codec's internal memory or guarantee throughput.

The focused owned-Mac test compiled actual recorder/router/admission/converter/broker sources with Swift 6 strict concurrency, `-O` and `-j2`. It passed with real AVAssetWriter files and video decoding:

- 24 original frames and PTS decoded correctly after the caller immediately mutated every source; all 24 chat-paint receipts survived copying.
- Actual 1080p queue capacity was 15, with the first 10,000-second source origin retained: 15 decoded frames and 25 queue drops from the 40-frame burst.
- A full private bank caused four actual broker capacity failures. The frozen four-drop receipt reached the final writer journal before one already completed private frame decoded after allocator closure. The independent raw capacity failure, provenance rejection, admission failure and invalid-PTS queue drop remained distinct.
- A fresh bank used a different actual IOSurface while the old exposed charge remained. Actual router rotation produced six old-file frames and one new-file frame with the expected source boundary. Stop-before-delayed-finish completed and decoded six frames.

The generated negative control restored only the original raw `appendVideo → enqueue` body. Its actual writer completed 24 frames with zero queue drops, and independent file decoding found all 24 pixels inverted by caller source reuse; fixed media retained all 24 originals. The optimized negative executable trapped with signal 5 and no diagnostic text. Its classifier exited 1 because assertion text was unavailable; this is retained rather than mislabeled as a fully classified negative-run pass.

Exact source, dependency, immutable Core framework, executable, log and media hashes are in [the dated receipt](RECORDING_PRIVATE_VIDEO_INGRESS_2026-10-22.json). These are post-run byte bindings, with all compiled-source modification times preceding the retained executable. The source-only controller, factory and ISO-worker changes were not compiled by this focused test. Combined shipping-app compilation and integrated controller/ISO media validation remain required before full vertical qualification. This evidence uses synthetic pixels and zero PCM, without capture, device output, network, physical performance or universal hardware qualification.
